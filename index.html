Noah-MP Optimization for Nepal

Thesis Research | Land Surface Modeling

Optimized the Noah-MP land surface model for improved soil moisture estimation through updated soil texture classifications and parameter calibration specific to Himalayan watersheds.

  • Enhanced soil moisture accuracy by updating soil texture database
  • Integrated ERA5 atmospheric forcing data for model inputs
  • Validated outputs against ground observations
  • Applied to agricultural and hydrological forecasting

Drought Monitoring Framework

Research Project | Climate Monitoring

Built a comprehensive drought monitoring and early warning system incorporating multiple drought indices and recovery period analysis.

  • Implemented SPEI and SMAI for meteorological and agricultural drought
  • Calculated soil moisture deficit relative to field capacity and saturation
  • Modeled recovery periods using skewed normal distributions
  • Integrated potential runoff analysis

Rice Land Suitability Analysis

Applied Research | Agricultural Planning

Developed methodology for rice suitability classification based on soil moisture requirements during on-season and off-season cropping windows.

  • Identified optimal cropping windows based on water availability
  • Created spatial suitability maps for rice cultivation
  • Informed irrigation infrastructure planning
